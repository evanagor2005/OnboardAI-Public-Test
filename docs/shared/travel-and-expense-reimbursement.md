## Request approval before booking

This process applies to HR and Software employees traveling for an approved business purpose, such as recruiting, training, or a technical conference. Submit the purpose, dates, destination, estimated cost, and budget owner through the private expense system at least five business days before domestic travel. International travel requests require at least 15 business days for Finance and IT review of payment and device arrangements.

The manager approves the business need and budget; Finance confirms the spending plan and any exceptions before booking. An invitation to an event is not travel approval. For urgent travel, request an expedited review before making a nonrefundable purchase rather than assuming the shorter notice is acceptable. HR coordinates accessibility or accommodation needs privately; medical details do not belong in an expense report.

## Book transportation and accommodation

Use the approved booking channel and choose standard economy airfare or an economical rail option appropriate to the business schedule. Finance must approve a higher travel class or an exception before purchase. Include necessary booking fees in the estimate and retain the booking confirmation.

The sample domestic hotel limit is USD 200 per night before taxes and mandatory property fees. Show those taxes and fees separately in the estimate. If suitable lodging is unavailable within the limit, obtain a documented exception before booking. Finance approves an itinerary-specific budget for international lodging rather than applying the domestic limit automatically.

Use public transit, a shared ride, or a taxi when practical. A rental vehicle requires advance manager and Finance approval. For an approved personal-vehicle trip, record the business route and distance; Finance supplies the applicable sample mileage rate for the travel date. Do not claim both mileage and fuel for the same personal-vehicle journey, and do not treat an ordinary commute as business mileage.

## Identify reimbursable expenses

Eligible expenses include approved business transportation, lodging, registration fees, and reasonable business meals. The sample domestic meal limit is USD 60 for a full travel day and USD 30 for a departure day, return day, or same-day trip. These are maximum reimbursements of actual spending supported by receipts, not automatic per diem payments. Meal totals include tax and tips; reimbursable tips are limited to 20 percent of the pre-tax meal amount. Finance approves international meal limits in the travel budget.

Do not claim a meal already provided by a conference, hotel, or another payer. Personal entertainment, alcohol, companion costs, minibar purchases, and optional personal upgrades are not reimbursable under this sample process. Separate personal items from business items on a mixed receipt. A company-card payment is recorded for reconciliation but is not reimbursed to the employee again.

## Submit receipts and currency details

Submit a travel expense report within 30 calendar days after returning from the approved business trip. Include the approved request reference, business purpose, expense date, category, amount, currency, and itemized receipt for each purchase. A card statement alone may establish payment but does not show which items were purchased.

For foreign-currency purchases, record the original amount and currency along with the posted USD amount from the payment record. If no posted conversion is available, Finance specifies the conversion method and supporting evidence. Do not apply several exchange rates to the same purchase to increase the claim.

Upload receipts only to the private expense system. Remove unrelated purchases and full payment-card numbers from supporting evidence while preserving the information needed to verify the expense. If a receipt is missing, first request a copy from the vendor; if unavailable, submit a missing-receipt declaration explaining the date, vendor, items, amount, and reason. Finance decides whether the evidence is sufficient. Late submissions require a written explanation and Finance review rather than an assumption of automatic rejection or approval.

## Review, payment, and corrections

Managers review complete reports within three business days of submission. Finance reviews them within five business days after manager approval. No employee approves their own report; a manager's own claim routes to the next authorized budget owner. A returned report identifies the required correction and resumes review when resubmitted.

For an approved employee-paid claim, Finance schedules reimbursement within 10 business days after final approval and sends a payment reference. Reimbursement is tracked separately from the biweekly wage schedule. Missing bank details or another payment block must be communicated to the employee with the action needed and revised timing. Update bank information only through the designated private portal described in [payroll guidance](../hr/payroll-and-timesheets.md), never through an email attachment or a chatbot.

Report a missing payment, duplicate claim, refunded booking, or incorrect amount to Finance with the report reference. Finance records corrections and explains any repayment or adjustment. Do not silently offset an error against an unrelated future expense or submit the same receipt to another team.
