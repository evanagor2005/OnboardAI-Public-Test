## Pay periods and submission deadlines

Cedar Works uses a biweekly sample payroll schedule. Each pay period runs from Monday through the second Sunday, with payday on the following Friday. The payroll calendar in the private employee portal identifies the actual period dates and any holiday adjustments.

Employees submit timesheets by noon on the Monday after the pay period ends. Managers approve them by noon Tuesday. These deadlines use the fictional Cedar Works office time zone, America/Denver, even when an employee works elsewhere. The published calendar identifies any earlier deadline needed for a holiday.

A missed submission or approval deadline requires immediate contact with Payroll. Managers should escalate a missing record rather than assume zero hours. Payroll determines the correction or additional payment process; a late record is not permission to omit earned pay.

## Recording work and leave

Hourly employees record actual start and end times and unpaid meal breaks each workday. Record all time worked, including required training and approved work outside the usual schedule. Do not estimate a standard shift when the actual hours differ, and do not record work during a meal break as unpaid time. Salaried employees record full-day attendance and leave using the categories assigned in their payroll profile; HR confirms any additional time-recording requirements for the role.

Use the appropriate work, PTO, sick, or holiday category. Approved leave should match the hours in the [time-off request](time-off-and-leave.md). For a partial-day absence, record worked time and leave separately without overlapping hours. If a category is missing, ask Payroll to resolve it before selecting an unrelated category.

Employees request manager approval before working additional hours when practical. If unplanned work occurs, record it accurately and notify the manager. Lack of advance approval does not justify removing worked hours. Payroll determines pay treatment from the employee's classification and applicable rules; this guide does not define overtime eligibility or rates.

## Manager review and corrections

Managers compare submitted records with known schedules, approved absences, and unusual entries. Ask the employee about discrepancies before approving. If an entry needs correction, return it with a specific explanation so the employee can update and resubmit it. Never approve or reject a timesheet solely to make a budget total match.

After payroll closes, report a correction through the private payroll support channel. Include the pay period, affected date, correct hours or category, and manager confirmation. Payroll preserves the original record and correction history, confirms any payment impact, and provides an expected resolution date. Do not move missing hours into a later period as though the work happened then.

## Reviewing a pay statement

On payday, review the pay period, gross pay, leave entries, deductions, and net deposit. Compare benefit deductions with the accepted [benefits enrollment](benefits-enrollment.md). An unfamiliar deduction should be checked with Payroll; a coverage-election issue also requires HR review.

If a deposit is missing or materially incorrect, contact Payroll promptly through the private support channel and state whether the issue creates an urgent payment problem. Payroll verifies the record, explains the next action, and confirms the correction timing. Do not post a pay statement, bank details, or tax identifiers in a team chat or chatbot conversation.

## Bank and tax information changes

Update bank and tax details only in the designated payroll portal using your own account. Payroll does not accept bank changes through ordinary email or a shared document. Complete the portal's verification process and check the effective pay date shown in the confirmation; a submitted change may not affect a payroll run already in progress.

If an unexpected message requests a bank change or asks for credentials, stop and report it through the security reporting channel. Payroll can verify the request through an established contact method without asking for a password or access code.
