## Core learning assignments

During [employee onboarding](employee-onboarding.md), HR assigns the core learning courses in the private learning system. In this sample program, all regular employees complete the following courses within 10 business days of their start date:

| Course | Expected learning outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Information security and phishing awareness | Protect accounts, recognize suspicious requests, and report a suspected incident |
| Privacy and confidential information | Use approved storage and sharing channels and limit unnecessary access |
| Respectful workplace and reporting concerns | Recognize inappropriate conduct and find the available reporting routes |
| Workplace safety and emergency procedures | Find location-specific safety instructions and know how to report a hazard |

Employees use their own invited accounts. A missing assignment should be reported to HR; a course completed under another person's account cannot establish the employee's completion. Contractors and other worker arrangements receive assignments appropriate to their access and duties after HR and the sponsoring manager review the scope.

## Role-specific preparation

HR staff complete employee-record handling training before receiving independent access to sensitive personnel records. Payroll access additionally requires payroll controls training and approval from the Payroll coordinator. Training completion is a prerequisite; the relevant system owner must still authorize access.

Interviewers complete structured interviewing and candidate privacy training before participating in any interview, even under supervision. New managers complete leave-request handling and performance feedback training within 30 calendar days of appointment. Until those manager courses are complete, HR arranges supervision for leave-request handling and performance feedback or assigns those activities to someone already qualified. Managers should not bypass preparation because a hiring or payroll deadline is approaching.

## Completion evidence and knowledge checks

A course is complete when the employee finishes the required material, submits any acknowledgment, and passes the course assessment. For courses with a scored assessment, the sample passing threshold is 80 percent. An attendance record or an opened page alone does not prove completion.

After an unsuccessful assessment, review the relevant material and retry. After two unsuccessful attempts, contact HR for guided support before another attempt. Report a technical submission failure with the course title, attempted completion time, and error text through the private support channel. Avoid screenshots containing employee records or credentials.

The learning system is the record of required-course completion. An onboarding learning path may link to a course, but marking that path step complete does not replace the course record. HR reconciles any discrepancy before reporting an employee as current.

## Renewals and changed source material

The four core courses renew every 12 months after completion. HR assigns a renewal 30 calendar days before the recorded completion expires. Role-specific refreshers are assigned when the relevant process changes or the course owner sets a renewal interval. The learning system shows the due date for each assignment.

A freshness warning on a learning path means its saved source material may need review; it does not automatically revoke a completed course or assign retraining. HR and the course owner decide whether a source change requires a new course version, an acknowledgment, or a refresher, and communicate any new deadline explicitly.

## Time, accessibility, and overdue assignments

Managers reserve working time for required training. Hourly employees record that time using the assigned work category in the [timesheet process](payroll-and-timesheets.md). Employees can request an accessible format, scheduling adjustment, or support privately through HR without sharing medical details with the team.

Contact HR before a due date if approved leave, an access failure, or another barrier prevents completion. HR records any revised deadline and coordinates coverage with the manager. A requested extension is not effective until confirmed. The learning system sends a reminder five business days before the due date; overdue assignments are reported to the employee and manager for a completion plan. Any access restriction requires a separate decision by the responsible system owner with HR coordination.

## Reporting concerns and tracking records

Report suspected phishing, exposed credentials, or unauthorized data access through the security reporting channel promptly; do not wait to finish training. Workplace conduct concerns can be raised privately with HR or the designated alternate reporting contact when the concern involves HR or the manager. HR identifies those contacts during orientation and in the private employee portal.

HR retains course version, assignment date, due date, completion date, and assessment outcome in the learning system. Managers see the completion status needed to support their teams. Detailed assessment responses and accommodation records remain limited to authorized staff. Completing a course does not replace reporting an incident or following the current source procedure.
