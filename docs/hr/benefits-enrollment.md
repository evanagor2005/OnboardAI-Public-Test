## Benefits eligibility and available coverage

In this sample program, Cedar Works offers medical, dental, vision, basic life insurance, and a retirement savings plan to regular employees scheduled to work at least 30 hours per week. HR confirms eligibility in the employee's private benefits profile. Temporary workers and contractors should ask HR about their arrangement rather than assuming the employee program applies.

Medical, dental, and vision coverage can include an eligible spouse or dependent children under the selected plan's terms. The enrollment portal shows available plans, covered services, employee contributions, and dependent eligibility. Employees should compare provider networks, deductibles, and prescription coverage before choosing; HR explains the enrollment process but does not choose a plan for an employee.

## New-hire enrollment window

Eligible new hires submit health coverage elections within 30 calendar days of their start date. For this sample program, coverage starts on the first day of the month following the start date once enrollment is accepted. A timely election submitted after that effective date may require a payroll adjustment for earlier coverage; HR confirms the effective date and any adjustment before the employee relies on coverage.

During the [first-week onboarding process](employee-onboarding.md), HR provides a private enrollment invitation and verifies that the employee can open it. The employee reviews personal details, selects coverage or records a waiver, adds eligible dependents if needed, and submits the election. Saving a draft does not submit it. Download the confirmation to personal secure storage and verify that each selected plan has an accepted status.

If the invitation is missing or the portal fails, contact HR before the deadline with the error message and the attempted submission date. Do not send identity documents or medical information through the company chatbot. HR records the access problem and explains the available resolution; an access issue does not itself confirm enrollment or an extension.

## Annual enrollment and life changes

HR announces an annual enrollment period each autumn for coverage beginning January 1. The announcement identifies the opening date, closing date, and whether existing elections carry forward. Employees review plan changes even when keeping the same coverage. Retirement contribution elections follow the retirement plan's separate instructions and are not restricted to the health plan's new-hire window.

Outside annual enrollment, report a possible qualifying life change, such as marriage, birth, or loss of other coverage, to HR within 30 calendar days of the event. HR verifies whether the event permits a change, identifies required supporting evidence, and confirms the effective date. Reporting an event does not automatically change coverage. Upload requested evidence only through the designated private benefits system.

## Payroll deductions and enrollment corrections

After enrollment, compare the confirmation with the benefits deductions on the first applicable pay statement. Coverage effective dates and deduction dates may differ. Report a missing plan, unexpected deduction, or dependent error to HR; Payroll handles deduction corrections after HR verifies the election.

Provide the plan name, affected pay date, and a short description through the private support channel. Do not place a complete pay statement or dependent identifiers in a shared message. Continue checking the portal until HR confirms the correction, and review the next statement using the [payroll and timesheet guidance](payroll-and-timesheets.md).
