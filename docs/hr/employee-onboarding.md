This is synthetic evaluation content. It contains no real employee information, legal requirements, or company policy. The dates are simulated source metadata, not Git commit timestamps.

## Before the employee starts

The hiring manager supplies HR with the start date, role, team, and equipment needs through the approved onboarding workflow. HR checks that the manager has identified a team contact and an onboarding buddy.

HR coordinates an account invitation with the appropriate administrator. A new employee joins a team through an invitation; knowing a team name is not sufficient to self-enroll. If the employee already has an account, the administrator should invite that account rather than create a duplicate identity.

HR records administrative preparation in the onboarding checklist. Personal identity documents and payroll details belong in the designated confidential HR system, never in shared onboarding documentation or chatbot conversations.

## First day

Confirm that the employee can sign in and see the learning paths assigned to them. Explain how to resume a path and how to open the original sources linked from a step. Ask the employee to report a broken source link rather than copy private information into a support chat.

Introduce the onboarding buddy and explain where to ask about equipment, access, and role-specific tasks. HR coordinates the experience; the employee's team administrator controls team learning-path publication and assignments.

## End of the first week

Check whether access problems are resolved and whether the employee has had an opportunity to work through the initial learning material. Progress belongs to the individual employee; one employee completing a shared path does not complete it for everyone else.

A later documentation change may produce a general warning for someone who already finished a path. The warning does not imply that their employment onboarding has been revoked. Direct questions about learning content to the owning team and questions about employee administration to HR.

## Knowledge check

What should HR do if an existing employee needs to join another team? Where should confidential employee records be stored? Why does one person's completion not remove a shared learning path?
