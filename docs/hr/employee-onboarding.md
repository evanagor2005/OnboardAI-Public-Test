## Onboarding is a guided learning path

Employee onboarding at Cedar Works is a sequence of practical learning steps that helps a new team member become ready for their role. The path introduces the people, tools, and team practices the employee will use during their first week. HR coordinates the shared welcome and access steps; each team supplies role-specific learning material.

Before the start date, the hiring manager confirms the employee's role, team, start date, team contact, and onboarding buddy. HR uses those details to coordinate an account invitation and assign the appropriate introductory learning path. If the employee already has an account, an administrator invites that account to the team instead of creating a duplicate identity.

## First-day learning and access

The employee signs in with their invited account and opens the learning path assigned for their role. HR confirms that the path is visible, introduces the onboarding buddy, and shows the employee how to resume a step and open its original source. The buddy helps explain team-specific material; the appropriate administrator handles access or assignment problems.

A learning step should point to useful source material, such as setup instructions, team practices, or a support contact. Employees should report a broken link to the owning team rather than copying private information into a support chat. Confidential identity documents and payroll details belong in the designated HR system, never in shared onboarding material or chatbot conversations.

## First-week progress

During the first week, the employee works through the initial learning steps with time to ask questions and try the relevant tasks. The onboarding buddy can clarify a step, while HR checks that access issues are resolved and that the employee knows where to find current source documents.

Learning-path progress belongs to the individual employee. One person's completion does not complete a shared path for colleagues. If a source changes after someone finishes, a freshness notice means the saved learning material may need review; it does not revoke the employee's onboarding or completion.
